About me

Surveyor · Lloyd's Register · PMP®

I am Yen-Jui Huang (Reay Huang), currently a Lloyd's Register (LR) Surveyor based in Ulsan, Korea, performing field surveys. Previously I spent more than 13 years at EVERGREEN LINE in full-time roles including Chief Engineer, Technical Superintendent, and Marine Engineer, covering both shipboard operations and shore-based fleet technical management.

I hold PMP® certification and Chief Engineer Certificates from the Maritime and Port Authority of Singapore (MPA) and the Maritime and Coastguard Agency (MCA UK). My graduate background combines naval architecture / marine mechatronic engineering with transportation and logistics management. This site collects maritime and management notes that can be shared publicly.
